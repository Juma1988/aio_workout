## 📖 About AIO Workout

**AIO Workout** is a comprehensive fitness tracking application designed to help you monitor and improve your health. Whether you're a beginner starting your fitness journey or an experienced athlete, AIO Workout provides all the tools you need in one elegant, easy-to-use app.

### 🎯 What Makes AIO Workout Special?

Unlike other fitness apps that focus on just one aspect of health, AIO Workout takes a **holistic approach** by combining:

- **Physical Activity** — Step tracking and workout programs
- **Hydration** — Smart water intake monitoring
- **Progress** — Visual analytics and achievement tracking
- **Motivation** — Notifications and streak tracking

Everything works together to give you a complete picture of your fitness journey.

### 🏃 Step Tracking

Your daily steps are more than just numbers — they're a measure of your activity and commitment to a healthier lifestyle.

**What You Get:**
- **Real-time Step Counting** — Watch your steps accumulate throughout the day
- **Interactive Charts** — See your daily, weekly, and monthly step trends
- **Goal Setting** — Set personalized daily step targets
- **Progress Indicators** — Visual rings and bars showing your daily progress

**Why It Matters:**
Regular walking reduces the risk of chronic diseases, improves cardiovascular health, and boosts mental well-being. AIO Workout makes it easy to stay on track.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites

- [Flutter SDK](https://flutter.dev/docs/get-started/install) (3.12.0 or higher)
- [Dart SDK](https://dart.dev/get-dart)
- [Android Studio](https://developer.android.com/studio) or [VS Code](https://code.visualstudio.com/)
- Firebase project (for Crashlytics)

### Installation

1. **Clone the repository**

```bash
git clone https://github.com/Juma1988/aio_workout.git
cd aio_workout
```

2. **Install dependencies**

```bash
flutter pub get
```

3. **Configure Firebase**

```bash
flutterfire configure
```

4. **Run the app**

```bash
flutter run
```

## 🏗️ Architecture

AIO Workout follows a clean, maintainable architecture designed for scalability.

<div align="center">

```
lib/
├── core/           # Core utilities, themes, and constants
├── data/           # Data layer and repositories
├── features/       # Feature modules
│   ├── achievements/
│   ├── dialogs/
│   ├── help_feedback/
│   ├── history/
│   ├── home/
│   ├── hydration/
│   ├── navigation/
│   ├── notifications/
│   ├── privacy/
│   ├── profile/
│   ├── program/
│   ├── splash/
│   └── steps/
├── l10n/           # Localization files
├── models/         # Data models
├── services/       # Business logic services
└── main.dart       # App entry point
```

</div>

### Why This Architecture?

- **Feature-based Structure** — Each feature is self-contained, making it easy to add new features or modify existing ones
- **Separation of Concerns** — Clean boundaries between UI, business logic, and data
- **Service Layer** — Reusable services for storage, notifications, and business operations
- **Provider State Management** — Efficient, predictable state management
